## Fonts these references use, and fit for ScanPay

| Font | Seen in | Free? | Fit |
|---|---|---|---|
| Inter Tight (current) | our book | Free, Google Fonts | Safe, very readable, a bit common |
| Suisse Intl | void. (#1) | Paid | Premium Swiss feel, closest to CRED-style |
| PP Mori / PP Neue Montreal | #29 | Paid (Pangram Pangram) | Very current fintech look |
| SF Pro | Payli (#11) | Apple only, can't license for web brand | Skip |
| Urbanist | board 2 specimen | Free, Google Fonts | Geometric, friendlier |
| Satoshi / General Sans | similar to #2, #3 | Free (Fontshare) | Modern, less common than Inter |
| Geist | similar to #7 | Free (Vercel) | Tech, clean, pairs with Geist Mono |

Pinterest titles and links were read directly from the search pages. #12, #18 and #29 had no clean pin link in the page data, so search them by name.
