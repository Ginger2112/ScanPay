01The idea

Two moments, one mark.

The four corner brackets are the frame a phone camera locks onto when it reads a QR code. The gold tick is what the buyer sees a few seconds later: paid. The whole product sits between those two moments, so the logo is just them, together.

The wordmark is lowercase and tight because it should feel calm, not loud. The gold full stop is the family signature shared with arun., which signs off the name the way a confirmed payment closes an order.

03Construction

Built on a 100-unit grid.

BracketsEach arm is 27.5 units long, stroke 8.75, round caps. The four arms never touch: the gaps are where the code sits.
TickThree points at (28,50), (44,66), (74,34). Stroke 11.25, heavier than the frame so the result reads first.
Clear spaceKeep empty space around the lockup equal to one bracket arm (about a quarter of the icon's width) on every side.
WordmarkHanken Grotesk ExtraBold, lowercase, tracking −5%. The full stop is always gold, never the same colour as the letters.
04Misuse

Don't.

✕ Recolour the tick

The tick is always gold. Red means a failed payment.

✕ Rotate or tilt

The frame stays square to the page, like a camera viewfinder.

✕ Stretch

Scale the icon evenly. Never squash it to fit a slot.

ScanToPay
✕ Retype the name

Always lowercase, ExtraBold, tight. Never camel case or caps.

✕ Busy backgrounds

Place it on black, the black gradient or paper only.

✕ Lose the gold stop

A white full stop breaks the link to the arun. family.

05Colour

Seven blacks, one gold.

The palette is mostly shades of black, so future products can share the scale and still feel related. Gold is the only brand colour. Mint and red are signals, used only for paid and failed. Click any swatch to copy its code.

06Type

One family, one mono.

Hanken Grotesk does everything with words: a calm Swiss-style sans that sets ScanPay apart from Arun's other products, which use Inter Tight. JetBrains Mono does everything with money and references, so amounts and UTRs line up and are easy to read back over the phone. Both are free on Google Fonts.

Rules

One gold button per screen. Pills, never square buttons. Amounts always show paise (₹899.00) and never more than the listed price.

Status marks come from the logo: the scan corners mean waiting, the tick means paid. Each status has its own shape, so it still reads without colour. No plain dots.
